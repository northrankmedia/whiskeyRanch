Our Story

The Whiskey Ranch Story: Two Words, One Place

Why We're Here

It's Right There In The Name

We wanted to create a place that felt less like another bar and more like your favorite place out in the country: comfortable, a little rough around the edges, full of character, and always ready for a good time.

Whiskey

The Spirit Of The Place

Good bourbon, cold drinks, live music, late nights, and the stories that somehow get better every time they're told.

Ranch

Something Bigger

In the South, the best places have always been gathering places: somewhere friends meet, strangers become friends, and everybody feels welcome enough to pull up a chair and stay awhile.

Put The Two Together

And You Get Whiskey Ranch

There aren't any cattle to round up. But there is plenty of whiskey. Whiskey Ranch was opened in February 2025 by Backwoods Hospitality Group, right here on the Oxford Square, to add a few stories of its own.

The Short History

Opened In February 2025

Whiskey Ranch opened in February 2025 at 1112 Van Buren Avenue in Oxford, Mississippi, on the Oxford Square. Backwoods Hospitality Group opened the bar to give the Square a place that feels like a favorite spot out in the country instead of another bar.

The name is the whole idea. The whiskey half covers good bourbon, cold drinks, live music, and late nights. The ranch half is the old Southern habit of making a gathering place, somewhere friends meet, strangers become friends, and everybody feels welcome enough to pull up a chair.

There aren't any cattle to round up, but there is a full bourbon and whiskey selection at the bar and live music every weekend on the Oxford Square. Look for the barrel out front.
