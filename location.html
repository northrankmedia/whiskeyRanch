Good To Know

Questions About Visiting Whiskey Ranch

Where is Whiskey Ranch in Oxford, MS?

Whiskey Ranch is at 1112 Van Buren Avenue, Oxford, MS 38655, on the historic Oxford Square. Look for the barrel out front.

What are Whiskey Ranch's hours?

Monday through Friday 4:00pm to 1:00am, Saturday 11:00am to 1:00am, and Sunday 11:00am to 9:00pm.

Is there parking near Whiskey Ranch?

Yes. Whiskey Ranch is an easy walk from Square parking and the University of Mississippi campus.

Does Whiskey Ranch have live music?

Yes. There is live music every weekend on the Oxford Square. To book the band for your own event, call (844) 643-3855 or email Info@backwoodshospitalitygroup.com.

What is on the menu at Whiskey Ranch?

Loaded fries, truffle fries, mozzarella sticks, chicken tenders, a chicken sandwich, a sausage plate, and four grilled cheeses, plus domestic and imported beer, vodka, bourbon, and tequila. Ask your bartender about the full bourbon and whiskey selection.

Do I need a reservation?

No. Walk-ins are welcome every night we are open.

Can I host a private party at Whiskey Ranch?

Call (844) 643-3855 or email Info@backwoodshospitalitygroup.com and we will get it sorted.

Do I have to be 21 to come in?

You must be 21 or older to purchase or consume alcohol at Whiskey Ranch, per Mississippi law.
