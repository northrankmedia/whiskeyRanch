Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026.

This page explains what information Whiskey Ranch collects through this website, and how it's used. We keep this simple: we're a bar & grill, not a data company, and we only collect what's needed to respond to you.

What We Collect

If you reach out through the email address or phone number on this site, we collect whatever you send us — your name, contact details, and your message. We use this only to respond to your question or inquiry.

We don't use tracking cookies, ad pixels, or analytics scripts on this site beyond what's needed to load the page itself (fonts and the embedded map).

Third-Party Services

This site embeds a Google Map to help you find us. Google's own privacy policy governs any information collected through that embed.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you send us only to respond to you. We don't sell your information to third parties, and we don't use it for anything beyond communicating with you.

Data Retention

We keep messages only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for basic recordkeeping. If you'd like your information removed, contact us using the details below.
