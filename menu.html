Menu

Bar Menu: Snacks & A Full Bar

Hand-cut fries, breaded tenders, grilled cheese with an attitude, and a bourbon list deep enough to keep you at the bar all night.

Kitchen & Bar

Food, Beer & Whiskey

Start with hand cut loaded fries or truffle fries, split an order of hand battered mozzarella sticks, or go for the in-house breaded chicken tenders. The grilled cheese lineup runs from the Cuban Melt to The Magnificent, built with a fried egg, smoked gouda, and honey-sriracha sauce.

Consuming raw or undercooked meat, poultry, seafood, shellfish, or eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness, especially if you have certain medical conditions. Prices and availability subject to change.

The Bar

Beer & Liquor

Cold domestics and imports, a vodka wall with flavors for days, and a bourbon shelf built for sipping slow.

Bourbon starts at $12 with Penelope, Tin Cup, and Russell's, steps up through Willett and Rebel Cask Strength, and tops out with Jefferson's Ocean at $20 and Booker's at $40. Vodka runs from Broken Shed at $10 to Grey Goose at $13, and Western Son comes in seven flavors. Tequila is mostly $12, with 1942 at $50.
